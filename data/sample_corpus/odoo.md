# Odoo ERP

Odoo is an open-source enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform with
integrated business applications. Organizations can manage sales, inventory,
accounting, purchasing, websites, and customer relationships in connected
modules. Shared records help teams follow a business process across departments.

Odoo installations can be extended with custom modules and configured for
different workflows. A reliable deployment should document its version,
dependencies, database backups, and upgrade procedure. Search over Odoo
documentation can help developers find model fields, module behavior, and
configuration guidance.
